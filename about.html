Our story

A neighbourhood salon built on skill, kindness and a love of great hair.

Since 2014

From a two-chair studio to your favourite salon

Velvet & Shears started with a simple idea: a salon should feel like a place you look forward to visiting. Founder Amelia Hart opened with two chairs, a handful of loyal clients and a big commitment to quality.

Today our team of four specialists serves thousands of clients every year, but the philosophy is unchanged — listen carefully, work precisely, and never rush.
